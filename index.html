About Kavya Fashion House

Welcome to Kavya Fashion House, your trusted local stitching center specializing in ladies wear and kids wear for girls. We are a family-run business dedicated to providing high-quality stitching and alteration services.

With years of experience in garment construction and alterations, we take pride in our attention to detail and commitment to delivering perfectly fitted clothing. From traditional Indian wear to modern western outfits, we handle all types of stitching with care and precision.

Whether you need a new outfit stitched from scratch or alterations to your existing garments, we ensure every piece fits you perfectly. We work with all types of fabrics and cater to customers of all ages and sizes.
